# B1 exam specification

The structure of the B1 Greek language exam of the University of Cyprus (UCY), School of Modern Greek: the parts, the tasks, the number of items, the points, the timing, the length of the texts and the audio, and the rules that matter for practice. The app and its content follow this document. The assessment criteria for writing and speaking are only named here; their full description, and the scoring of reading and listening, is in [assessment-criteria.md](assessment-criteria.md). The IDs of the task types, and what a content item of each type must give, are in [task-types.md](task-types.md); the topics, in [topics.md](topics.md).

## How to read this document

UCY publishes only a short description of the exam: the parts, the points, the task types and the timing ([ucy-structure]). It publishes no sample papers, no item counts and no criteria (see [docs/sources/README.md](sources/README.md)). The KEG (Centre for the Greek Language, Thessaloniki) publishes all of these for its own B1 exam, and the UCY task descriptions are the KEG ones word for word, without the numbers (compare [ucy-structure] with [keg-structure]). So the gaps are filled from KEG.

Every fact has a basis:

| Basis | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **UCY** | A UCY document says so. |
| **KEG** | A KEG document says so, and UCY says nothing on the point. We assume UCY does the same. |
| **Samples** | Seen in the two KEG B1 sample papers (series A and B). Word counts marked so are our own counts. |
| **Estimate** | Our estimate; the reason is given. |
| **Unknown** | No source; listed in [Gaps](#gaps). |

The source documents are linked by short names; the list is at the end. How the KEG exam itself differs from the UCY one, for the fallback, is in [The KEG exam](#the-keg-exam-the-fallback).

## The exam at a glance

| Part | Greek name | Points | Time | Tasks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Listening | Κατανόηση προφορικού λόγου | 25 | 30 min | Unknown at UCY; 2 at KEG |
| Reading | Κατανόηση γραπτού λόγου | 25 | 50 min | 4 |
| Writing | Παραγωγή γραπτού λόγου | 25 | 70 min | 2 texts |
| Speaking | Παραγωγή προφορικού λόγου | 25 | 12 min per pair | 3 parts |

- **Points and timing:** UCY ([ucy-structure]). KEG gives the same points but less time: listening 25, reading 40, writing 55 min ([keg-structure]).
- **The written exam** (listening, reading, writing) takes 2 h 30 min in total, with no break (UCY: [ucy-structure], [ucy-regs] B.3). Listening comes first, and nobody enters or leaves the room during it (UCY: [ucy-regs] B.2). The order of reading and writing after it is unknown; KEG puts listening and reading in one booklet and writing in a second one (KEG: [keg-guide-en] p. 18).
- **Whether the 2 h 30 min is one block** in which the candidate divides the time between reading and writing, or each part is timed separately: unknown.
- **Speaking** is a separate session: candidates arrive 10 min before it, and 45 min before the listening (UCY: [ucy-regs] B.1).
- **The pass mark:** at least 60% in each of the four parts, that is 15 of 25 points. Failing one part fails the whole exam (UCY: [ucy-regs] C.1–2).
- **The result:** the certificate shows the final score on a scale of 0–100 (UCY: [ucy-regs] C.3), which is the sum of the four parts. The final exams of the UCY courses, which have the same structure, report grades on a 0–10 scale: Καλώς 6.0–6.49, Λίαν καλώς 6.5–8.49, Άριστα 8.5–9.49, Άριστα με διάκριση 9.5–10 (UCY: [ucy-final] 4.5).
- **Same exam for course students and independent candidates:** the same structure and difficulty, and the same certificate (UCY: [ucy-info]).

### Rules for all parts

| Rule | Basis |
|---|---|
| Blue ink only. Errors are crossed out with one line; no correction fluid. | UCY ([ucy-regs] B.8, B.11) |
| No dictionaries or other printed or electronic aids. | UCY ([ucy-regs] B.8) |
| Rough paper is given and handed in with the booklet; rough notes do not count. | UCY ([ucy-regs] B.12, [ucy-final] 3.7) |
| The candidate's real name must not appear anywhere in the writing. | UCY ([ucy-structure], [ucy-regs] B.13) |
| Write in small letters, not only in capitals, in writing and in notes. | KEG ([keg-guide] p. 22–23); the KEG B1 writing scale counts writing in capitals against the text ([keg-writing]). |
| Give exactly as many answers as the task asks for. More answers can make the whole task score zero. | KEG ([keg-guide] p. 27) |
| Each reading and listening task starts with a worked example, item 0, which earns no points. | Samples |
| Spelling counts little at the lower levels, but fully phonetic spelling is not accepted. | KEG ([keg-guide] p. 26) |

## Listening (Κατανόηση προφορικού λόγου)

| Property | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Points | 25 | UCY ([ucy-structure]) |
| Time | 30 min (KEG: 25 min) | UCY ([ucy-structure]) |
| Texts | Audio on the situations taught at B1: friendly to fairly formal texts, informative texts, extracts from interviews or literary works, dialogues. | UCY ([ucy-structure]) |
| Task types | Notes; comparison (table completion); true/false; gap fill in a text; multiple choice with 3 options. | UCY ([ucy-structure]) |
| Number of tasks | Unknown. KEG: 2 tasks, notes (12 points) and comparison (13 points). | KEG ([keg-structure]) |
| Total length | 600–700 words for the whole part | KEG ([keg-structure]) |
| Replays | Each text is played twice. | KEG ([keg-guide] p. 26); stated in the instructions of every sample task |
| Audio length | The series B recording runs 20 min 25 s: two texts, each played twice, with the instructions and the pauses. | Samples ([sources-index]) |
| Answers | Written in the booklet while listening. | Samples |

UCY lists five task types, but KEG uses only two of them at B1, and both KEG samples have exactly those two. Which of the five a UCY paper uses, and how the 25 points are split, is the largest gap in this document. The KEG pair (notes and comparison) is the most likely core; the app trains all five types.

### Notes (Σημειώσεις)

| Property | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Items | 12 + the example | KEG ([keg-structure]) |
| Points | 12, 1 per item | KEG ([keg-structure]) |
| Text | Friendly or informative monologue, a narration of events, an extract from an interview or from a literary work; about 300 words. | KEG ([keg-structure]) |
| Sample texts | An author reads from his book about his grandfather's life (A, 280 words); a radio program about Arcturos, a project that protects bears (B, 310 words). | Samples ([keg-a-listening], [keg-b-listening]) |
| Answer | A form of 12 labeled lines in the order of the text; the candidate writes a short note of 1–3 words (A) or 1–4 words (B). | Samples |
| What is asked | Facts: years, ages, numbers, places, jobs, short phrases ("Ηλικία του παππού, όταν ήρθε στην πόλη: 12 χρονών"). | Samples |
| Accepted answers | The key lists alternatives where the text allows them ("ορεινό / σ' ένα βουνό της Πελοποννήσου", "πήγαν σχολείο / σπούδασαν"). | Samples |

### Comparison, table completion (Σύγκριση – συμπλήρωση πίνακα)

| Property | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Items | 13 ticks + the example | KEG ([keg-structure]) |
| Points | 13, 1 per tick | KEG ([keg-structure]) |
| Text | A fairly formal dialogue, a discussion between 2–3 people; 350–400 words. | KEG ([keg-structure]) |
| Sample texts | A radio discussion with two members of environmental organizations (A, 385 words); four job interviews (B, about 310 words). | Samples ([keg-a-listening], [keg-b-listening]) |
| Answer | A grid with one column per person or organization (2 in A, 4 in B) and one row per statement (11 in A, 6 in B). The candidate ticks every column that the statement fits. Some statements fit more than one column. The instructions give the total number of ticks. | Samples |

### True/false, gap fill and multiple choice

UCY names these types for listening; KEG does not use them at B1, so there is no sample. The format is assumed by analogy (estimate):

- **True/false (Σωστό/Λάθος):** as in reading, statements with a tick under ΣΩΣΤΟ or ΛΑΘΟΣ, 1 point each.
- **Gap fill in a text (Συμπλήρωση κενών σε κείμενο):** a short text or summary with gaps, filled with words heard in the audio.
- **Multiple choice with 3 options (Πολλαπλή επιλογή με 3 επιλογές):** a question or a sentence start with options α, β, γ, one of them right.

Items and points: unknown.

## Reading (Κατανόηση γραπτού λόγου)

| Property | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Points | 25 | UCY ([ucy-structure]) |
| Time | 50 min (KEG: 40 min) | UCY ([ucy-structure]) |
| Tasks | 4, in this order: matching, comparison, true/false, gap fill. Points 6 + 6 + 6 + 7. | UCY lists the four types ([ucy-structure]); order, items and points KEG ([keg-structure]) |
| Total length | 600–700 words of text | KEG ([keg-structure]) |
| Texts | Authentic texts, adapted; the source is named under each text. | UCY ([ucy-structure]: "Κείμενο αυθεντικό"); Samples |
| Answers | Numbers or ticks written into a table in the booklet. No writing of words. | Samples |

### 1. Matching sentence halves (Σύνδεση/Αντιστοίχιση προτάσεων/ενοτήτων)

| Property | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Items | 6 + the example, and 3 distractors | KEG ([keg-structure]) |
| Points | 6, 1 per item | KEG ([keg-structure]) |
| Text | Authentic, fairly formal: informative text; 150–180 words. | UCY for the type ([ucy-structure]); length KEG ([keg-structure]) |
| Sample texts | Tips for teaching children to ski (A); tongue-in-cheek tips for mothers on getting the family to pack (B). With all the halves, the tasks run to 220 and 236 words. | Samples ([keg-a-reading], [keg-b-reading]) |
| Answer | The text has numbered sentences (0–6) that break off after their first part. A table holds 10 second parts: the example, the 6 right ones and 3 distractors. The candidate writes the number of the first part next to the second part that completes it. | Samples |
| Distractors | Plausible continuations that fit the grammar but not the meaning or the context. | Samples |

### 2. Comparison, table completion (Σύγκριση – συμπλήρωση πίνακα)

| Property | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Items | 12 ticks + the example | KEG ([keg-structure]) |
| Points | 6, 0.5 per tick | KEG ([keg-structure]) |
| Text | Authentic, formal: descriptive or advertising text, results of a survey, a simple historical extract, simple biographies; 150–180 words. | UCY for the type ([ucy-structure]); length KEG ([keg-structure]) |
| Sample texts | Five short adverts of new restaurants (A, 198 words); five short adverts of travel agencies (B, 250 words). | Samples ([keg-a-reading], [keg-b-reading]) |
| Answer | A grid with one column per text (5) and one row per feature (7). The candidate ticks every text that offers the feature. Each feature fits 1–3 texts; the instructions give the total number of ticks (12). The example is one tick in the first row; that row can hold a scored tick too (B). | Samples |

### 3. True/false (Σωστό/Λάθος)

| Property | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Items | 6 + the example | KEG ([keg-structure]) |
| Points | 6, 1 per item | KEG ([keg-structure]) |
| Text | Less formal: an extract from a narrative or from a literary work; 180–200 words. | UCY for the type ([ucy-structure]); length KEG ([keg-structure]) |
| Sample texts | Ancient Greek theater, from the introduction of a school textbook (A, 209 words); an extract from a novel, Ζωρζ Σαρρή «Όταν ο ήλιος» (B, about 170 words). | Samples ([keg-a-reading], [keg-b-reading]) |
| Answer | A tick under ΣΩΣΤΟ (agrees with the text) or ΛΑΘΟΣ (does not). There is no "not given" option. | Samples |
| False statements | They change a detail of the text (once a year instead of four times; barefoot instead of high shoes) or draw a wrong conclusion from it (no female roles, because only men acted). | Samples |

### 4. Gap fill from a word bank (Συμπλήρωση κενών)

| Property | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Items | 7 gaps + the example, and 6 distractors | KEG ([keg-structure]) |
| Points | 7, 1 per gap | KEG ([keg-structure]) |
| Text | A friendly text, a simple newspaper or magazine article that informs on a topic; 180–200 words. | UCY for the type ([ucy-structure]); length KEG ([keg-structure]) |
| Sample texts | Drinking water and health (A, 195 words); guide dogs for the blind (B, 150 words). | Samples ([keg-a-reading], [keg-b-reading]) |
| Answer | A bank of 14 words or short phrases: the example, the 7 right ones and 6 distractors. The candidate writes the number of the gap next to the word that fits it. | Samples |
| Distractors | Mostly other forms of the right answers or of the words around the gap: πολλή / πολλά, να δούμε / να δουν, στην καθημερινή / στην καθημερινότητα. The task tests grammar as well as meaning. | Samples |

## Writing (Παραγωγή γραπτού λόγου)

| Property | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Points | 25: part A 12, part B 13 | UCY for the total ([ucy-structure]); the split KEG ([keg-structure]) |
| Time | 70 min for both parts (KEG: 55 min) | UCY ([ucy-structure]) |
| Length | Part A 150 words. Part B: no length given by UCY; 150 words at KEG. 300 words in total. | UCY for part A ([ucy-structure]); KEG for part B and the total ([keg-structure]) |
| Tolerance on the length | No rule. The KEG B1 scale describes a text in the band 11–15 of 25 as "fairly long, but with some shortfall in the number of words", so a short text loses points. | KEG ([keg-writing]) |
| Handwriting | In the booklet, in blue ink, in small letters. | UCY ([ucy-regs] B.11); KEG for small letters ([keg-guide]) |
| Name | Never the candidate's own name. The KEG tasks say: do not sign with your name, use another one. | UCY ([ucy-structure]); Samples |
| Scoring | A holistic scale of 25 points in five bands of 5 points (KEG B1). How it gives the 12 and 13 points of the two parts, and what UCY uses, is unknown. | KEG ([keg-writing]) |

### Part A: an informal text (Α΄ μέρος)

- **Text types:** a friendly letter, an extended announcement, an extended advertisement, and so on (UCY: [ucy-structure]).
- **Samples:** a letter to a friend who cannot find a job, with advice (A); a letter to a friend about the hiking club you joined (where you go, what you do, whom you met, how you feel), persuading them to join (B) ([keg-a-writing], [keg-b-writing]).

### Part B: a formal text (Β΄ μέρος)

- **Text types:** a formal text that gives personal or other information, asks for leave with reasons, complains with reasons, and so on (UCY: [ucy-structure]).
- **Samples:** a letter of complaint to a bank manager, after the bank called about a late loan payment (A); a magazine article that advises parents on how to help their children choose a profession (B) ([keg-a-writing], [keg-b-writing]). So part B is not always a letter.

### The prompt (both parts)

From the samples ([keg-a-writing], [keg-b-writing]):

- A situation in 3–5 sentences, in the second person plural: who you are, what happened, to whom you write and why. Sometimes a list of the points to cover, in brackets.
- The required length, "(150 λέξεις)", and the warning about the name.
- The start of the text, printed in the answer space: a date line ("… Μαΐου"), the salutation ("Αγαπημένε/η μου …,", "Αγαπητέ/Αγαπητή …,", "Κύριε Διευθυντά,") and the first words ("Σου γράφω για να σου πω …", "Σας γράφω αυτή την επιστολή …"); or, for an article, its title.

## Speaking (Παραγωγή προφορικού λόγου)

| Property | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Points | 25 | UCY ([ucy-structure]) |
| Time | 12 min per pair for the three parts (an older UCY version of 2024 gave 15 min) | UCY ([ucy-structure]; [sources-index] for the older version) |
| Candidates | In pairs. KEG: with an odd number, one group of three; a single candidate plays part C with the examiner. | UCY for pairs ([ucy-structure]); KEG for the rest ([keg-speaking-instr]) |
| Examiners | Two; for course students, never their own teachers. KEG: an examiner who leads the talk and an assessor; each grades alone. | UCY ([ucy-course]); KEG ([keg-speaking-instr]) |
| Recording | Audio-recorded, for re-evaluation. | UCY ([ucy-regs] B.7) |
| Preparation | None, except 1–2 min in part C to read the role (KEG: about 2 min). | UCY ([ucy-structure]) |
| Flow | A friendly talk; the examiner does not announce the parts, and moves from one to the next as naturally as possible. | KEG ([keg-speaking-instr]) |
| Time per part | Unknown. Estimate: A about 2 min, B about 5 min (2–3 min per candidate), C about 4 min plus the time to read the roles, so that the total is 12 min. | Estimate |
| Scoring | KEG: five criteria of 1–5 points each, total 25: grammatical accuracy, pragmatic appropriateness, pronunciation and intonation, vocabulary, communicative effectiveness. UCY: unknown. | KEG ([keg-speaking]) |

### Part A: simple questions (Α΄ μέρος)

- **Content:** simple questions about name, residence, school or work, family, hobbies (UCY: [ucy-structure]); a warm-up (KEG: [keg-structure]).
- **Samples:** 7–8 questions that the examiner asks each candidate: name, where you live, school or work, free time, whether you have visited Greece and have friends there, other languages, favorite food ([keg-a-speaking], [keg-b-speaking]).

### Part B: a short monologue (Β΄ μέρος)

- **Content:** a short monologue in continuous speech, more demanding than at the lower levels. If photos are given, the candidate is asked about the feelings that the photo shows and gives reasons (UCY: [ucy-structure]).
- **Two halves** (KEG: [keg-speaking-instr]): each candidate answers (a) one question that asks for a short developed answer, and (b) questions about a picture, a different one for each candidate.
- **The opinion question** asks for a view with reasons. Samples: is TV good or bad for children; shopping centers or small shops; how to protect the environment; how to prevent forest fires; do you recycle; does the climate shape character; do schools teach enough; does music affect mood; the good and bad sides of the internet; how to keep good relations with others; how to reduce litter ([keg-a-speaking], [keg-b-speaking]).
- **The picture:** the candidate looks at it for 1 min and then describes it. The examiner's sheet has follow-up questions: what you see, where the people are and how they feel, then personal questions on the topic (news and journalism, computers at school, gym and health, TV). The examiner may ask helping questions when the candidate gets stuck (Samples).

### Part C: role play (Γ΄ μέρος)

- **Content:** a role play between the two candidates, more demanding than at the lower levels, with a good deal of argumentation (UCY: [ucy-structure]).
- **Role cards:** each candidate gets role A or role B on a separate sheet and does not see the partner's role. The candidates read the roles themselves; the examiner steps in only on a misunderstanding (KEG: [keg-speaking-instr]).
- **The card:** 3–7 sentences in the second person plural: the situation, your position and your reasons, the partner's position, and often "you insist" (Samples).
- **Situations in the samples:** mostly a disagreement to argue out between friends, relatives or colleagues (a comedy or a historical film; a group tour or an independent trip; a work uniform; online or printed newspapers; a family car or a sports car; a cheap phone or an expensive one; an online language course). Some are service encounters with a disagreement (a clerk who recommends the gym over the park; a shop assistant who recommends a new computer over an upgrade), one is a plain request for information (swimming lessons at a municipal pool), and one is joint planning (a school's end-of-year party) ([keg-a-speaking], [keg-b-speaking]).
- **Register:** informal (εσύ) between friends, formal (εσείς) with a clerk or an official (Samples).

## Consequences for the app

- **Mock exam (story 14):** the UCY timing (listening 30, reading 50, writing 70 min), listening first, no pause between parts; speaking on its own. A part passes at 15 of 25.
- **Listening (story 7):** in exam mode each text plays twice, without pause or seeking. Practice mode may allow more.
- **Reading content (story 6):** the four KEG tasks with their counts: 6 + 3 distractors, 12 ticks over a 5 × 7 grid, 6 true/false, 7 gaps + 6 distractors; each with an example item 0.
- **Task instructions** state the number of answers, as the exam does, and the app checks that exactly that many are given.
- **Writing (stories 10, 11):** two texts of about 150 words with a word counter; the start of each text is given; a name to sign with is given, never the user's own.
- **Speaking (story 13):** the app plays the examiner in parts A and B and the partner in part C, with a hidden role card for itself; 1–2 min to read the role and 1 min to look at the picture.

## The KEG exam, the fallback

The fallback is the KEG B1 certificate ([README.md](../README.md#the-exam)). The UCY exam copies the KEG task design of 2012, on which this document is built, so the two differ less in content than in timing, results and calendar. What is the same: four parts of 25 points, the four reading task types, the two writing parts and their genres, the three speaking parts in pairs, 12 min per pair, and the pass mark of 60% in each part, where one failed part fails the whole exam ([keg-guide] p. 22, [ucy-regs] C.1–2).

### The calendar

The results of the UCY January 2027 session come by mid-April 2027, before the naturalization deadline of 2027-06-14 ([README.md](../README.md#the-exam)). KEG cannot meet that deadline: the October 2026 session was in Greece only and took place on 2026-10-23; the next one in Cyprus is in May 2027, with results at the end of July. So KEG is no second chance before the deadline. After it, KEG May 2027 is the earliest retake (results at the end of July, against about November for the UCY September 2027 session), and the way in if no UCY place is won on 2026-11-02.

### What the app would need for KEG

- **Content:** nothing new. Every KEG task type is a UCY task type, and the content follows the KEG counts of 2012.
- **Mock exam (story 14):** a KEG profile with 25, 40 and 55 min and the listening pair notes + comparison. The KEG times are 17–21% shorter than the UCY ones, so the profile is also a harder practice for the UCY exam. A setting, not a feature.
- **The new comparison format:** if a KEG paper or sample confirms it, both comparison tasks need a variant with one answer per statement. That is the `choice` answer format, which the true/false tasks already use ([task-types.md](task-types.md#answer-formats)), with the texts or speakers as the options and new counts for the validator: 6 statements × 4 texts, 10 statements × 2 speakers. Until then the app keeps the 2012 grid.
- **The answer sheet:** the time to copy the answers onto it counts against the KEG time limit. The app does not simulate the sheet; a mock exam in the KEG profile can remind the user to leave a few minutes for it.
